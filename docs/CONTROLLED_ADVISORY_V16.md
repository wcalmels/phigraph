# PhiGraph v1.6 Controlled Advisory Mode

Version 1.6 adds a governed advisory queue on top of shadow deployment.

## Capabilities

- review queue with explicit approval or rejection;
- SLA due dates and breach status;
- permission policies by autonomy level and action type;
- simulation of reversible actions;
- maturity promotion checks;
- non-execution guarantee.

## Maturity promotion

Promotion requires sufficient shadow and labeled cases, precision, controlled
false positives, operator acceptance, readiness score, and complete audit
coverage.

No operational action is executed by this version. Approved advisory cases are
records for a future controlled execution layer.
